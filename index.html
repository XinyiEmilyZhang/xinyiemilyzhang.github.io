About

I’m Xinyi Zhang, a PhD candidate in Biostatistics at NYU Grossman School of Medicine, advised by Iván Díaz and Michele Santacatterina.

I develop statistical methods for estimating causal effects across populations, with applications in clinical trials and population health. My research interests include causal inference, transportability, causal machine learning, and treatment effect heterogeneity.
